Directed the R&D strategy for a Generative AI platform designed to revolutionize the pre- and post-examination patient journey. The system utilizes Large Language Models to analyze natural language patient complaints, performing automated clinical triage to route patients to the appropriate specialty and assess eligibility for telemedicine, optimizing scheduling and follow-ups. Supported by the TUBITAK 1501 program. (2024-Present)
