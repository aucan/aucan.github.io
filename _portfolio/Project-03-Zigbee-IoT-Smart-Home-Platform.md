Architected and developed a comprehensive smart home ecosystem for Gesislab Elektronik, supported by the TUBITAK 1501 program. Designed a centralized, web-based command hub comparable to industry leaders like Google Home, enabling seamless wireless device orchestration via the Zigbee protocol. Managed a heterogeneous technology stack, integrating low-level firmware (C, C++) with dynamic backend services (Python, C#.Net) and a hybrid database layer (PostgreSQL, NoSQL). (2015-2018)
