Directed the R&D and AI strategy for an indigenous robotic system designed to automate high-risk chemotherapy drug preparation, mitigating critical safety risks including contamination and foaming. The system ensures end-to-end medication traceability by integrating directly with Turkey's national health ecosystems (ITS and e-Prescription). Supported by the TUBITAK 1501 program. (2023)
