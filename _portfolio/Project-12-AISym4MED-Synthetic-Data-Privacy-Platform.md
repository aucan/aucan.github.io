Contributing to a major EU-funded initiative (Horizon Europe) designed to address data scarcity and privacy bottlenecks in medical AI. The project is developing a trustworthy platform that generates high-fidelity synthetic medical datasets via controlled data synthesis, integrating novel anonymization pipelines and attribute-based privacy measures to provide a compliant, scalable data ecosystem for healthcare data engineers and practitioners. (2023-Present)
