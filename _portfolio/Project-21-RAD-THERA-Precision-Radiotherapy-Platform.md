Directed the R&D strategy for a deep-learning-based radiotherapy planning system designed to automate precise segmentation of Target Tumor Volumes and Organs-at-Risk. Leveraging multi-modal imaging (CT/MRI), the platform optimizes radiation dose distribution to maximize therapeutic efficacy while minimizing toxicity, reducing manual contouring workload and inter-observer variability. Supported by the TUBITAK 1501 program. (2024-Present)
