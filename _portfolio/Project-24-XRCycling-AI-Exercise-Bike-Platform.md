Directing the R&D strategy for an immersive, technology-driven sports ecosystem designed to combat obesity and facilitate tele-rehabilitation. The platform transforms stationary cycling into an interactive experience via Virtual Reality and gamification, using multi-modal AI that fuses facial expression analysis (fatigue detection) with EMG muscle activity sensors to drive a personalized "Sport Bot" for adaptive vocal coaching and remote clinical monitoring. Supported by the TUBITAK 1501 program. (2025-Present)
