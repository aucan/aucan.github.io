Spearheaded the R&D strategy to transform legacy, passive Personal Health Records (PHR) into a proactive, AI-driven health management ecosystem. The platform leverages Graph Machine Learning to model patient-disease relationships and family history for chronic disease risk stratification, and Reinforcement Learning to optimize the timing and content of personalized health interventions. Supported by the TUBITAK 1501 program. (2024-Present)
